Prism Pricing Strategy v1

Positioning, Pricing Psychology & Revenue Strategy

⸻

Core Observation

The current pricing structure is strong for an early-stage SaaS and demonstrates a clear understanding of customer segmentation.

However, the biggest opportunity is not changing the prices themselves.

The biggest opportunity is shifting pricing away from usage limits and closer toward business value delivered.

Customers do not buy:

* AI
* Sentiment Analysis
* Dashboards
* Reports

Customers buy:

* Faster decision-making
* Better consumer understanding
* Reduced research costs
* Faster client delivery
* Competitive advantage

The pricing page should reflect this reality.

Future Pricing Evolution

Current pricing is analysis-based.

Long-term, Prism should evolve toward a credit system.

⸻

Why Credits Scale Better

Not all analyses are equal.

Examples:

Small CSV
Cost: Low

10,000 reviews
Cost: Medium

100-page PDF
Cost: Higher

Call transcript archive
Cost: Higher

⸻

A credit system allows pricing to scale naturally.

Example:

* 100 Credits / Month
* 500 Credits / Month
* Custom Enterprise Credits

This prevents large users from consuming disproportionate resources.

Final Conclusion

Prism should position itself as a premium intelligence platform rather than a low-cost analytics tool.

The strongest path is:

1. Launch quickly.
2. Get first paying customers.
3. Validate willingness to pay.
4. Improve reports relentlessly.
5. Increase pricing as value becomes proven.

The first goal is not maximizing revenue.

The first goal is proving that customers repeatedly use Prism because it helps them make better decisions faster.

Once that is validated, pricing power naturally follows.
